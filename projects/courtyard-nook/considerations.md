  - Rest on wall and wall-drilled-pins
  - Mixture
    - Standalone stilts for front two and back right
    - L-shaped rests at three points in the wall: corner, then at both extents of the flat wall.
- Airflow for AC unit/Heat pump
- Depth:
  - need to fit a bike's length + (ideally) room for eventual heat pump
